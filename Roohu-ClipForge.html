Settings

The AI needs two free keys. Everything else is optional.

🔑 AI keys

With the key, captions understand any spoken language (Urdu, Hindi, Arabic, English and 90+ more) with exact word timing. Without it, the app uses its offline English and Hindi/Urdu models.

OpenRouter free models (names ending in :free) share 20 requests a minute and 50 a day for your whole account (1,000 a day after you buy $10 of credits). One Auto Create run uses about 8 requests. Free models are run by outside companies, and some may keep or train on what they receive, so use them only for videos you are fine sharing. Models marked 👁 can look at the pictures. Others get the caption text only.

Internet needed when the AI works (Auto Create, “Title and rate with AI”, “Plan again with AI”). The AI you choose gets the caption text and 4 small pictures from each clip. Your full video and your audio are never uploaded. Ratings describe how strong an opening is. They do not predict views.

Clips

Face tracking runs on your phone and uses no Gemini limit. It works best on upright, front-facing faces. If no face is found, the single AI crop is used. Speed ramps are skipped while face cam cutaways are on. The editor preview stays at normal speed; ramps appear in the exported clip.

Captions
Caption accuracy
Captions transcribe the words spoken in the audio; they do not describe silent visuals. For best results, choose the exact spoken language and use the Groq Whisper key in Settings. Offline mode supports English and Hindi. Urdu and other languages need Groq Whisper or an imported .srt file. Always review names, slang and unclear speech in the caption editor.
Cinematic Color Studio

Color grading is applied during phone export. Set strength to 0 for the original look. Existing clip effects and captions stay separate.

Look of the clip

The preview in the editor stays plain. The look appears in the exported clip. Both looks use the title of each clip. Neon story types the title letter by letter in glowing green, with the first line in yellow.

Face cam cutaways

Add a second video, for example a face reaction. The exported clip then cuts between your main video and this one, like a reaction edit. The sound stays from the main video. Every cut gets a zoom and a flash. Phone export only.

Music

Add tracks you have the rights to use, and tag each by mood. Clips are matched to the mood the analysis detects (calm, upbeat or intense). Tracks last for this session.

Smoothly bring music in and fade it out at the end of each exported clip.

Set to 0 for music-only clips. 100 keeps the original sound level.

A gentle noise gate lowers very quiet background hiss between speech. It is not AI noise removal and may soften quiet words.

Music Finder (YouTube)

The AI reads the selected clip (mood, topic, what is said) and picks the best real songs for it and finds them on YouTube. You preview the best pick, then add that song on YouTube with Add sound when you upload. The app never downloads or rips audio from YouTube videos.

YouTube upload

To upload after every export, use the switch on the Post screen (step 3).

Setup: in Google Cloud Console make a project, turn on “YouTube Data API v3”, create an OAuth client of type Desktop app, add your Google account as a test user, then paste the Client ID and secret here and tap Connect. Google keeps uploads from a new, unaudited project private until you pass their API audit, and each upload uses 1600 of the 10,000 free daily quota units (about 6 uploads a day). Keys stay encrypted on this phone.
